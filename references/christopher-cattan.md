## Good Chart Conditions

Cattan says a 15-figure shield chart can be called good and well-constructed for three reasons:

1. It contains many good figures.
2. It has more even-point figures than odd-point figures.
3. Its total point count exceeds 96, or at least reaches 96.

Use this as Cattan-specific chart quality language. Do not present it as the only universal validity rule.

## Eight Ways a Chart Becomes Fortunate or Unfortunate

Cattan evaluates fortune or misfortune through eight factors:

| Factor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Nature and condition | Whether the figures naturally agree, share the same element, or are identical. |
| Attribute | Whether a figure matches the subject being asked about. |
| Place | Whether a figure falls in a good, middling, or bad house. |
| Aspect | Whether a good or bad figure sees another by a favorable or unfavorable aspect. |
| Adjacent relation | Cattan discusses this through companionship, but the core skill omits Company content per user instruction. |
| Change | Whether a good/bad or direct/retrograde contrast alters the outcome. |
| Motion | Whether the first figure or significator repeats in other houses and where it moves. |
| Generation | Whether the figure is naturally generated, artificially generated from mothers/daughters/nieces/witnesses/judge, or generated through triplicity; also consider whether it comes from good or bad parents. |

When teaching, focus on the usable sequence: figure nature -> house -> aspect -> motion -> generation.

## Masculine and Feminine Logic

Cattan uses both house polarity and element polarity:

- Odd houses are masculine: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11.
- Even houses are feminine: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12.
- Fire and air figures are masculine.
- Water and earth figures are feminine.

If a figure's gender, element, and house fit the querent or question, Cattan treats this as strengthening the reading.

## Motion of Figures

Cattan gives great weight to motion: when the 1st-house figure appears again in another house, read the meaning of the house it enters.

Basic motion meanings:

| 1st figure moves to | Cattan-style meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 2宫 | The querent seeks help, outcome, or concern through money, goods, or possessions. |
| 3宫 | Through siblings, kin, messages, or short journeys. |
| 4宫 | Through father, land, houses, inheritance, buried things, or the end of the matter. |
| 5宫 | Through children, pleasure, pregnancy, games, or messengers. |
| 6宫 | Through servants, illness, labor, or small animals. |
| 7宫 | Through spouse, partner, opponent, lawsuit, or open enemy. |
| 8宫 | Through death, fear, partner's goods, inheritance, or loss. |
| 9宫 | Through travel, religion, dreams, studies, or long journeys. |
| 10宫 | Through rulers, office, career, judgment, or public honor. |
| 11宫 | Through friends, allies, hopes, or assistance. |
| 12宫 | Through prison, hidden enemies, confinement, sorrow, or large animals. |

If the first figure does not move, Cattan reads it as the matter standing by its own judgment and not seeking outside help.

## Good and Bad Houses

Cattan emphasizes house strength:

- Angular houses: 1宫、4宫、7宫、10宫. These are strongest, public, present-tense, and show things happening now or requiring effort.
- Succedent houses: 2宫、5宫、8宫、11宫. These are middling, partly public and partly hidden, and tend toward future development.
- Cadent houses: 3宫、6宫、9宫、12宫. These are weak, hidden/private, delayed, or connected to the past.

Good figure in good house: doubly good.

Bad figure in bad house: doubly bad.

Good figure in bad house: promises some good but cannot preserve it fully; it reduces some harm.

Bad figure in good house: brings harm, but the good house mitigates part of it.

## 占有

Occupation / 占有 occurs when the querent's figure, usually the 1st-house figure, moves directly into the house of the matter asked about.

- If the occupying figure is good, this is one of the strongest favorable signs.
- If the occupying figure is bad, it is a bad sign.
- Judge by the nature of both the figure and the occupied house.

Use the user's established terminology: 占有, not "占居" as the main term.

## Figure Matching the Question

Cattan sometimes calls a chart or figure well-matched when the figure suits the question or the querent:

- A masculine querent with a masculine first-house figure is better matched.
- A feminine querent with a feminine first-house figure is better matched.
- 道路 in a travel question is naturally fitting.
- 获得 in a gain/profit question is naturally fitting.

Explain this as "卦与问题相合", not as a separate required doctrine if the user did not ask for Cattan specifically.

## Aspects

Cattan's general aspect logic:

- 合相: figures agree by element and/or planet, especially when good figures are involved.
- 六合: counted at a favorable interval from the reference house.
- 刑照: a hard interval, generally troublesome when bad figures are involved.
- 拱照: a favorable interval.
- 冲照: opposition, often difficult or contrary.

When a good figure appears in a good aspect, judge favorably. When a bad figure appears in a bad aspect, judge unfavorably.

Keep this separate from 精准相位. Cattan's aspect rules are general aspect doctrine; 精准相位 is the specific perfection doctrine already preserved in `interpretation.md`.

According to the user's technical genealogy, Cattan represents a Renaissance expansion of geomantic aspect doctrine. Use this framing when teaching Cattan:

- Cattan uses 占有 and 结合 as the main conjunction/perfection-like forms, while expanding general aspects such as 六合, 三分/拱照, 刑照, and 冲照.
- His aspect judgment does not merely count geometry. It evaluates whether the relevant figures support or damage the 1st-house figure and the quesited figure.
- Three comparison factors matter in Cattan-style aspect reading: the first/top row of the figures, planetary agreement, and elemental agreement.
- A favorable aspect with poor figure agreement is weakened; a difficult aspect between figures of the same element, planet, or compatible nature may become less hostile.
- The 1st house is emphasized because it signifies both the querent and the beginning/omen of the matter.

Do not present Cattan's aspect expansions as mandatory for every modern reading. Use them when the user asks for Cattan, Renaissance technique, or technical history.

## 意图点 and 精神点

Cattan's original point de l'intention is 意图点. In the user's modern framework, this belongs to the broader family of 盘眼 techniques: methods that extract the hidden question, theme, or interpretive center from the chart.

When explaining this to users, distinguish three layers:

1. **盘眼**: the user's functional Chinese term for the wider cross-cultural technique of finding the chart's hidden question-core.
2. **意图点**: Cattan's historical European form, used mainly when the question is unknown, concealed, unclear, or deliberately withheld.
3. **精神点**: a modern improvement/reinterpretation of Cattan's 意图点, used to examine hidden inner factors, mental/spiritual orientation, belief, intention, and subtle support.

Cattan gives three methods for identifying the likely intention of a chart when the question is unknown:

1. First check whether the 1st-house figure moves into another house; use that house topic as the likely intention.
2. Add the odd points across the twelve house figures.
3. Distribute that count through the houses in order.
4. The house where the count stops indicates the likely topic. This is the core point-counting form that later supports the modern 精神点.
5. Combine the 1st and 7th figures, then inspect the resulting figure and its place in the chart as the querent-object relationship.
6. If it stops on the 15th figure, judge by the 法官; if using witnesses and judge, distribute their odd points similarly.

Cattan says this method is optional and not necessary to geomancy. The user interprets this as evidence that Cattan preserved the method but did not fully understand or foreground its broader pan-geomantic function.

When a user asks about 精神点, explain:

- Its historical root is Cattan's 意图点.
- Its modern meaning is no longer only "what question is hidden?" but "what hidden inner, mental, spiritual, or intentional factor organizes the chart?"
- It pairs with 福点: 福点 leans toward body, wealth, resources, material condition, and worldly support; 精神点 leans toward mind, belief, spiritual condition, inner motive, and subtle aid.
- For the user's fuller explanation, refer them to the user's public account article at 源生迹义: https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/NNy5rF4S-XrDWQ_mcsPQpA

Do not present 精神点 as a medieval Cattan term. Present it as a modern development from Cattan's 意图点.

## 点之路

Cattan's 点之路 traces the 法官 backward through matching top-row points.

Basic procedure:

1. Take the first/top point of the 法官.
2. Compare it to the first/top point of the 右证人.
3. If it does not match, compare it to the 左证人.
4. If neither witness matches, there is no 点之路.
5. If a witness matches, trace backward to the connected 侄女卦, then to the matching mother/daughter houses.
6. The house where the trace lands becomes a key testimony for the whole question.
7. A single point is treated as stronger than a double point for this technique.

Use the user's term 点之路. If source text says "point track", "line of points", or "行迹法", normalize to 点之路.

## 福点

Cattan's Part of Fortune should be translated as 福点. Do not use 福德分点 as the main term.

福点 is calculated from the total points of the twelve house figures:

1. Add the total points in the twelve house figures.
2. Divide by 12.
3. Use the remainder as the house of the 福点.
4. If the total divides evenly, place it in the 12th house.

Examples of remainder placement:

- Remainder 2 -> 2宫.
- Remainder 4 -> 4宫.
- Remainder 6 -> 6宫.
- Remainder 8 -> 8宫.
- Remainder 10 -> 10宫.
- Remainder 0 -> 12宫.

Judge both the house and the figure there:

- Good figure + good house = favorable.
- Bad figure + bad house = unfavorable.
- Mixed figure/house = moderate.

Cattan mentions an alternate method using the twelve houses plus witnesses and judge, but says he did not find it reliable. Do not use the alternate method unless discussing source variants.

When comparing 福点 with 精神点:

- 福点 is a later European development from the same broader 盘眼-style point projection logic, but it no longer primarily asks "what is the hidden question?"
- 福点 is used after the ordinary judgment to supplement the chart with material fortune, worldly resources, body/condition, support, and practical benefit.
- 精神点 keeps the hidden/inner emphasis of 意图点, but modernizes it into an indicator of mental, spiritual, intentional, and subtle factors.

## Triplicities

Cattan uses elemental triplicities as a judgment layer:

- If three relevant figures are of one element and one nature, judge by that triplicity's condition.
- If the chart is split between triplicities, judge by the stronger triplicity and its element/planetary condition.

Cattan gives these day/night triplicity rulers:

| Element | Day ruler | Night ruler |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 风 | 太阳 | 木星 |
| 火 | 水星 | 月亮 |
| 水 | 金星 | 火星 |
| 土 | 土星 | 火星 |

## Planetary Hour Check

Cattan gives a chart-quality check by planetary hour:

- Find the ruler of the hour when the question was asked or when the chart is cast.
- Check whether one of that planet's figures appears in the four angular houses.
- If yes, Cattan treats the chart as well made and fit for judgment.
- If no, he says the chart should be dismissed and cast again.

This conflicts with the user's earlier removal of judge validity checks but is not the same rule. Present it only as Cattan's optional hour-based check, not as a required validity rule.

## Placing Zodiac Signs Above Figures

Cattan has a technique for placing zodiac signs over each figure according to the figure's zodiacal correspondence, then using the added zodiac layer after the main geomantic judgment.

Use this only as a secondary Cattan-specific layer. Do not let zodiac placement override the main figure, house, motion, witness, 法官, or 精准相位 analysis.

## Figure Nature and Direction of Motion

Cattan says figures whose upper points exceed lower points are entering/inward and generally fortunate, except 悲伤.

Figures whose lower points exceed upper points are exiting/outward and generally unfortunate, except 喜悦.

Use this as a Cattan-specific tendency, not a replacement for the user's main figure meanings.

## Nativity

Cattan's nativity rule:

- If the 1st house and 5th house are ruled by the same planet or contain figures of the same planetary type, they describe the person's temperament and constitution.
- Also observe where the 1st-house figure moves: if it moves into a good house, it supports fortune; if into a bad house, it shows difficulty.

Use this only when the user asks about Cattan's personal/nativity method.
