# Curriculum

Use this module to teach European geomancy in a staged way.

## Stage 1: Figures

Goal: recognize the sixteen figures and remember their core images.

Practice:

- Drill Latin name, English image, and Chinese gloss.
- Compare figure pairs: Via/Populus, Acquisitio/Amissio, Puer/Puella, Caput/Cauda Draconis.
- Ask the learner to interpret one figure in three different question contexts.

## Stage 2: Generation and Arithmetic

Goal: form a valid chart without calculation errors.

Practice:

- Generate four mothers from odd/even marks.
- Derive daughters by rows.
- Combine figures by parity.
- Practice row-by-row point calculation until the learner can generate a complete shield chart.

## Stage 3: Shield Chart Reading

Goal: read 右证人, 左证人, 法官, 调解者, and 点之路.

Practice:

- Summarize 右证人 and 左证人 as testimony in the matter.
- Read the 法官 as the symbolic verdict.
- Trace 点之路 only after the basic shield chart positions are understood.

## Stage 4: House Chart Reading

Goal: connect figures to topics through houses.

Practice:

- Identify querent and quesited houses.
- Assess house condition and relationships.
- Use 精准相位 and aspects to decide whether contact occurs.

## Stage 5: Techniques

Goal: apply specialized methods only when the question calls for them.

Topics:

- 精准相位.
- Triads.
- Timing.
- Lost objects.
- Weather.
- 福点 and 精神点.
- Secondary figures.

## Stage 6: Correspondences

Goal: use correspondences as interpretive support, not as a substitute for chart logic.

Topics:

- Elements.
- Planets.
- Zodiac signs.
- Lunar mansions.
- Directions.
- Body and subtle-body symbolism.
- Personality correspondences.

## Stage 7: Advanced Technical Material

Goal: refine technical reading without adding magical, devotional, or spiritual-worldview material.

Topics:

- Letter and name techniques.
- Secondary figures.
- 福点 and 精神点 as chart factors, including their relationship to 卡坦的意图点 and the user's 盘眼 framework.
- Comparative correspondence systems.
- Modern reconstruction only when it clarifies technical chart reading.

## Teaching Rule

Do not teach advanced techniques before the learner can produce and read a basic shield chart.
