# Scope

## Include

- Western/European geomancy as a divinatory art.
- Sixteen geomantic figures and their meanings.
- Figure structure, names, alternate names, realms, planetary synthesis, gendered naming discussions, and related correspondences.
- Figure generation by hand, random methods, apps, cards, and parity.
- Shield charts, house charts, 右证人, 左证人, 法官, 调解者, and chart mathematics.
- Western interpretive techniques: 精准相位, triads, 点之路, affirmation, fortunateness, reverse questions, aspects, timing, lost objects, weather, 福点, 精神点, and secondary figures.
- Astrological and elemental correspondences: planets, zodiac signs, lunar mansions, directions, body correspondences, personality typing, Part of Fortune/福点, Part of Spirit/精神点.

## Exclude

- Feng shui and non-Western spatial geomancy.
- General divination essays that are not specifically about geomancy.
- Geomantic emblems, badges, chaplets, and devotional emblem systems.
- Book reviews and resource-review posts in the core corpus.
- Geomantic magic, ritual practice, devotional material, spiritual worldview, and historical-origin speculation.
- Unrelated occult, astrological, or magical material unless it directly explains a technical geomantic correspondence or chart-reading method.

## Borderline Handling

If a topic mixes geomancy with other systems, keep only the technical geomantic rule, correspondence, or chart-reading method. State that spiritual, magical, devotional, or historical-worldview material is outside scope.

If the user asks for a complete corpus, include a "retained" and "excluded" section so the omissions are visible.
