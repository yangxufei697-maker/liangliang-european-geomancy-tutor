# Correspondences

Use this module for elemental, planetary, zodiacal, directional, and body correspondences. Do not present correspondences as one universal table. Western geomancy preserves multiple systems and competing attributions.

## Use Rule

Always state the system being used when a correspondence differs by tradition.

Good phrasing:

- "Using the traditional elemental-ruler grouping..."
- "Using the Agrippan/planetary zodiacal method..."
- "Using Gerard of Cremona's zodiacal assignment..."
- "Treat this as comparative or reconstructed material, not a universal rule."

Do not combine planetary, zodiacal, elemental, directional, and body correspondences into a deterministic diagnosis. Use them as descriptive layers after the chart's significators and 精准相位 are read.

## Figure Rows

The four rows of a geomantic figure are usually read top-to-bottom:

| Row | Element | Body image | Function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fire | Head | will, spirit, intention, heat, rising |
| 2 | Air | Throat/chest | breath, speech, movement, mediation |
| 3 | Water | Belly | feeling, digestion, fluidity, reception |
| 4 | Earth | Feet/base | body, matter, grounding, weight |

One point indicates the element is active. Two points indicate the element is passive.

## Elemental Rulers

Use this as the core elemental-ruler table:

| Element | Figures |
| --- | --- |
| Fire | Laetitia, Cauda Draconis, Fortuna Minor, Amissio |
| Air | Rubeus, Puer, Coniunctio, Acquisitio |
| Water | Albus, Puella, Via, Populus |
| Earth | Tristitia, Caput Draconis, Carcer, Fortuna Major |

Teaching notes:

- Laetitia, Rubeus, Albus, and Tristitia each have only one active line and are clean examples of Fire, Air, Water, and Earth respectively.
- Populus has no active line but is grouped with Water through its relationship with Via and its receptive, collective nature.
- These groups are useful for memory, direction, weather, temperament, and technical comparison.

| Planet | Figures |
| --- | --- |

| Mercury | Albus, Coniunctio |
| Venus | Puella, Amissio |
| Mars | Puer, Rubeus |
| Jupiter | Acquisitio, Laetitia |
| Saturn | Carcer, Tristitia |
| Dragon's Head | Caput Draconis |
| Dragon's Tail | Cauda Draconis |

Reading notes:

- Planetary pairing gives temperament and motive, not the whole answer.
- Paired figures under one planet often differ by stability/mobility, benefic/malefic expression, or inward/outward mode.
- Caput and Cauda Draconis work as nodal/threshold forces more than ordinary planets.

## Directions

Directional systems differ. Use directions mainly for lost-object, location, weather, or space-oriented questions, and name the system.

### Element-Derived Direction Rule

If no specific historical direction table is requested, derive directions from the element system being used. A common modern/Agrippan-style mapping is:

| Direction | Element | Figures by core elemental ruler |
| --- | --- | --- |
| East | Air | Rubeus, Puer, Coniunctio, Acquisitio |
| South | Fire | Laetitia, Cauda Draconis, Fortuna Minor, Amissio |
| West | Water | Albus, Puella, Via, Populus |
| North | Earth | Tristitia, Caput Draconis, Carcer, Fortuna Major |

### Historical Variants

Historical variants include Fludd and Arabic manuscript direction systems. Use those only when the user asks for that specific system or when reconstructing a technical historical method.

Do not quietly swap direction systems during a reading.

## Body Symbolism

Use body symbolism cautiously and symbolically. It is not medical diagnosis.

Firm row-level body symbolism:

| Row | Element | Body zone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Fire | head |
| Air | Air | throat/chest/breath |
| Water | Water | belly/stomach/navel |
| Earth | Earth | lower body/base/feet |

Useful figure examples:

- Laetitia, with only Fire active, fits the head.
- Rubeus, with only Air active, fits the throat.
- Albus, with only Water active, fits the belly or stomach area.
- Tristitia, with only Earth active, fits the lower body or base.
- Via may indicate navel/lower belly, with some ambiguity.
- Populus and Coniunctio have uncertain torso-related readings in the discussed manuscript tradition.

For health questions, frame these as symbolic study correspondences and recommend professional care for real symptoms.

## Reading Guidance

Correspondences work best as secondary descriptors:

1. Identify significators.
2. Assess 精准相位.
3. Use figure meaning.
4. Add correspondences only to refine tone, location, body area, timing, or descriptive symbolism.

Examples:

- Lost object: use house, figure, elemental direction, and nearby descriptors.
- Weather: combine figure quality with element and moisture/heat symbolism.
- Body question: use row/figure body symbolism carefully, not as diagnosis.
- Comparative study: name the exact attribution system and avoid harmonizing contradictions away.
