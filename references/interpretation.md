# Interpretation

## Reading Order

For a full practical reading:

1. Clarify the question and identify the relevant house or significator.
2. Identify the relevant figures: querent, quesited, 右证人, 左证人, 法官, and 调解者 if used.
3. Check house placement and 精准相位.
4. Examine triads, aspects, 点之路, and special techniques only when relevant.
5. Consider timing, lost objects, weather, or derived figures only when the question calls for them.
6. Give a concise outcome and one study-style recommendation.

## 精准相位 / Perfection

Use 精准相位 for binary or connection-based questions: "Will X happen?", "Can I obtain X?", "Will I meet/contact/recover X?"

Do not use 精准相位 as the main tool for open questions such as "Where is X?", "What is X?", "When will X happen?", or "What kind of person is this?" Those need location, timing, figure, house, or descriptive techniques.

精准相位 shows whether relevant realities connect. It does not by itself show whether the result is pleasant, fortunate, wise, or desirable. Assess the quality through the figures, houses, aspects, and question context.

### Historical Logic

According to the user's research, European 精准相位 developed from early attempts to translate astrological conjunction into geomantic structure. Since every geomantic house contains a figure, geomancy could not copy astrological conjunction directly. It instead used two simpler ideas:

- **相同**: the same figure appears in the querent's house and the quesited's house, or repeats in a relevant place.
- **相邻**: the querent figure, quesited figure, or a linking figure appears next to the relevant house.

The user calls the older adjacent-house logic **伴卦**. Later medieval authors narrowed and formalized some of this material into the four 精准相位.

When explaining the history, say:

- 12th-century material used conjunction-like logic before the later technical doctrine fully crystallized.
- 13th-century authors such as Pietro d'Abano formalized the main forms: 占有, 结合, 变位, 转化, along with broader aspect ideas.
- 精准相位 is therefore not merely "aspect" in the ordinary astrological sense; it is a geomantic connection doctrine built from repetition and adjacency.

Keep 伴卦 separate from the removed Company material. 伴卦 may be mentioned as a historical/technical precursor to 精准相位, but do not rebuild the deleted Company taxonomy.

### Four Main Types

- **占有 / Occupation**: the same figure appears in both significator houses. Strongest 精准相位; the matter is already joined or naturally aligned.
- **结合 / Conjunction**: one significator figure passes to a neighboring house of the other significator. The passing side makes the approach or bears more of the work.
- **变位 / Mutation**: both significators pass to each other's neighboring houses. Both sides move, and the connection may come through change or mutual adjustment.
- **转化 / Translation**: a third figure links the significators. A third party, mediator, circumstance, or shared channel brings the connection.

### 无精准相位

无精准相位 means the chart does not show the needed connection. It does not automatically mean "bad"; sometimes a denied event is desirable, protective, or relieving.

Use this matrix:

| 精准相位状态 | Figures favorable | Figures difficult |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 有精准相位 | The event connects and tends well. | The event connects but may be troublesome. |
| 无精准相位 | The event does not connect, likely to the querent's benefit or without harm. | The event does not connect and the broader condition remains difficult. |

### Multiple Significators

For complex questions, test 精准相位 between all meaningful pairs. A medical question, negotiation, legal matter, or business matter may need querent, other party, treatment/action, object, and outcome houses. Build a qualified answer instead of reducing the chart to one yes/no.

### Reverse Questions

For negative or inverse questions, first rewrite the query into a clean affirmative form. 精准相位 answers whether the stated event connects; it does not handle double negatives elegantly.

Example: change "Will I not lose the job?" into "Will I keep the job?" before judging 精准相位.

## Triads

Triads group figures into meaningful clusters for summary. Use them after significators and 精准相位, not before.

Good use:

- Summarize the flow from root to action to result.
- Compare the querent's side and quesited's side.
- Notice repeated elemental, planetary, or qualitative themes.

Bad use:

- Replacing the 法官.
- Forcing a narrative when the question only needs 精准相位.

## 点之路 / Way of the Point

点之路, or Via Puncti, traces an active line from the 法官 upward through its generating figures. Traditionally the Fire line is emphasized as the line of will, intent, desire, and motivating force.

Use it to identify the root cause or driving impulse behind the 法官. It is especially useful when the result is clear but the cause or source of pressure is unclear.

Basic procedure:

1. Start with the 法官.
2. Choose the active line to trace. Prefer Fire unless the tradition or question gives a reason to trace another line.
3. Move upward to the parent figure that contains the active line.
4. Continue through 证人, 侄女卦, and 母卦/女儿卦 levels until reaching a top-tier figure.
5. Interpret that top-tier figure as a root cause, motive, or origin testimony.

If the path branches, is ambiguous, or does not clearly support the question, state that the chart's causal testimony is weak or mixed.

## Aspects

Use aspects when figures are placed in houses. Assess cooperation, conflict, obstruction, or ease according to house relationship and figure condition.

Do not treat aspects as a full replacement for 精准相位 unless the tradition being used explicitly does so. Aspects can soften, describe, or complicate a chart, but the main 精准相位 judgment remains separate.

Common reading:

- Favorable aspect with 精准相位: the connection is easier or more harmonious.
- Difficult aspect with 精准相位: the connection happens with friction.
- Favorable aspect without 精准相位: there may be opportunity or sympathy, but not enough connection by itself.
- Difficult/no aspect without 精准相位: reinforces separation or difficulty.

## Timing

Timing is conditional and tradition-dependent. Give timing as a symbolic estimate, not a guarantee. State the method used.

## Lost Objects

For lost-object questions, first decide whether the question is "Can I recover it?" or "Where is it?"

- For recovery: prioritize 精准相位/aspect between querent and object significators.
- For location: prioritize the object's significator, house placement, direction, figure nature, and nearby descriptors.

Recovery clues:

- 占有: the object was never far from the querent, or is easy to recover.
- Querent's figure passes: the querent finds it through effort.
- Object's figure passes: it returns with little effort from the querent.
- 变位: it appears unexpectedly.
- 转化: another person or circumstance brings it back.

House quality can describe speed or effort: angular is quicker, succedent slower, cadent delayed or difficult.

## Weather

For weather, use figure qualities and elemental symbolism. Keep forecasts symbolic and short unless the user asks for a worked method.

## Answer Template

Use this compact structure when the user asks for an interpretation:

1. **问题核心**: relevant house/significator and main figure.
2. **连接状况**: 精准相位, aspect, or 无精准相位.
3. **盘面证词**: 右证人, 左证人, 法官, 调解者, triads, 点之路, or special method if relevant.
4. **结果倾向**: likely symbolic outcome.
5. **建议**: one practical or study-oriented next step.

## Source Anchors

- Affirmation and fortunateness: 精准相位 connects significators but does not decide good/bad by itself.
- Complex perfection: complex questions may require multiple significator pairings.
- Inverse questions: rewrite inverse questions cleanly and use 精准相位 only for suitable yes/no questions.
- 点之路: traces the motivating/root cause from the 法官 upward.
- Lost objects: use 精准相位 for recovery and house/figure clues for location.
