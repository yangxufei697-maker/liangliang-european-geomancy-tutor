# Figures

Use this module for figure meanings and correspondences. In ordinary answers, prefer Chinese figure names. When explaining a single figure, give both the English name and Chinese name.

Point patterns are written left-to-right to represent top-to-bottom rows. `1` means a single point; `2` means a double point. Use these patterns for chart generation and point calculation.

When giving a concrete example of a single figure's structure, first state the numeric pattern, then optionally show the points from top to bottom. Use point diagrams only in examples, not in every ordinary figure reference.

Example:

```text
男人 / Puer: 1121
 ·
 ·
· ·
 ·
```

## Core Figure Set

- **道路 / Via**: movement, change, travel, instability, passage.
- **群众 / Populus**: multitude, reflection, crowd, passivity, diffusion.
- **大吉 / Fortuna Maior**: strong success, inner support, durable favorable condition.
- **小吉 / Fortuna Minor**: quick success, external help, temporary benefit.
- **结合 / Coniunctio**: joining, meeting, exchange, negotiation, crossroads, union.
- **限制 / Carcer**: containment, restriction, delay, binding, structure.
- **悲伤 / Tristitia**: sorrow, heaviness, decline, depth, grief, slow maturation.
- **喜悦 / Laetitia**: elevation, happiness, increase, celebration, rising.
- **女人 / Puella**: harmony, beauty, softness, pleasure, diplomacy.
- **男人 / Puer**: force, conflict, impulse, heat, cutting action.
- **白色 / Albus**: clarity, wisdom, calm, purity, counsel.
- **红色 / Rubeus**: passion, disruption, vice, danger, intoxication, volatility.
- **获得 / Acquisitio**: acquisition, profit, increase, accumulation.
- **失去 / Amissio**: release, expense, loss, sacrifice, letting go.
- **龙首 / Caput Draconis**: entrance, beginning, increase, threshold.
- **龙尾 / Cauda Draconis**: exit, ending, decrease, release.

## Teaching Notes

- Treat figures as symbolic actors, not fixed yes/no tokens.
- Read a figure by question context, chart placement, condition, and relationships.
- Be careful with auspiciousness: a "bad" figure may be useful for ending, cutting, binding, or loss; a "good" figure may be unhelpful when the question requires separation.
- Use Chinese names in ordinary reading. Include English and Chinese names together when teaching a single figure.

## Figure Cards

Use these cards for quick readings. Always modify by house, significator role, 精准相位, and question context.

### 道路 / Via

- **Core**: movement, road, change, journey, transition, instability.
- **Helpful for**: travel, relocation, leaving, changing course, opening a path.
- **Difficult for**: permanence, fixed agreements, stability, keeping things unchanged.
- **Reading tone**: things move; the answer may be "not settled yet."
- **Misread warning**: Via is not automatically good because it opens movement. If the question asks for staying, holding, or securing, it can show restlessness or loss of fixity.

### 群众 / Populus

- **Core**: crowd, collective, receptivity, reflection, multiplicity, diffusion.
- **Helpful for**: public matters, groups, popularity, absorbing influence, waiting.
- **Difficult for**: decisive action, individual will, clarity, uniqueness.
- **Reading tone**: many voices or conditions are involved; the matter reflects its surroundings.
- **Misread warning**: 群众 can be neutral and passive. Do not force it into a strong yes/no without support from the broader chart.

### 大吉 / Fortuna Maior

- **Core**: greater fortune, durable success, inner strength, protected achievement.
- **Helpful for**: long-term gain, recovery, authority, stable victory, strong foundations.
- **Difficult for**: quick pivots, light matters, situations needing flexibility.
- **Reading tone**: favorable, especially when persistence and inner resources matter.
- **Misread warning**: It is not instant luck. It often favors effort that has roots.

### 小吉 / Fortuna Minor

- **Core**: lesser fortune, quick success, external favor, temporary advantage.
- **Helpful for**: short-term wins, help from others, public favor, quick opportunities.
- **Difficult for**: long-term stability, private strength, lasting control.
- **Reading tone**: favorable but not deeply rooted.
- **Misread warning**: It can pass quickly. Check whether the chart supports durability.

### 结合 / Coniunctio

- **Core**: joining, meeting, exchange, union, crossroads, negotiation.
- **Helpful for**: contact, agreements, commerce, messages, mediation, collaboration.
- **Difficult for**: separation, solitude, keeping matters apart.
- **Reading tone**: things connect or cross paths.
- **Misread warning**: Joining is not always harmonious. It can also show entanglement, compromise, or being caught at a crossroads.

### 限制 / Carcer

- **Core**: prison, enclosure, restriction, containment, delay, binding.
- **Helpful for**: protection, boundaries, discipline, holding something in place, imprisonment of a harmful influence.
- **Difficult for**: freedom, travel, release, growth, easy movement.
- **Reading tone**: the matter is contained, delayed, blocked, or made rigid.
- **Misread warning**: Carcer is not always bad. In questions about security, vows, storage, or stopping harm, it can be useful.

### 悲伤 / Tristitia

- **Core**: sorrow, heaviness, decline, grief, depth, burden.
- **Helpful for**: humility, descent, seriousness, hidden/deep matters, accepting hard truth.
- **Difficult for**: joy, speed, confidence, increase, celebration.
- **Reading tone**: heavy, slow, sad, or lowering.
- **Misread warning**: It may show necessary gravity rather than total failure. Check whether the question benefits from depth or restraint.

### 喜悦 / Laetitia

- **Core**: joy, elevation, rising, celebration, uplift, openness.
- **Helpful for**: happiness, increase, morale, celebration, visible improvement.
- **Difficult for**: secrecy, sobriety, staying low, deep concentration.
- **Reading tone**: upbeat, rising, encouraging.
- **Misread warning**: Joy is not the same as completion. It can show good spirits before the matter is secured.

### 女人 / Puella

- **Core**: beauty, harmony, softness, pleasure, diplomacy, attraction.
- **Helpful for**: love, art, reconciliation, peace, beautification, social grace.
- **Difficult for**: conflict, hard decisions, cutting ties, discipline.
- **Reading tone**: pleasant, conciliatory, aesthetic, relational.
- **Misread warning**: Softness can also mean avoidance, vanity, or lack of force when force is needed.

### 男人 / Puer

- **Core**: force, heat, conflict, impulse, courage, cutting action.
- **Helpful for**: competition, surgery/cutting, attack, initiative, urgent action.
- **Difficult for**: peace, patience, diplomacy, careful planning.
- **Reading tone**: hot, martial, fast, aggressive.
- **Misread warning**: Puer gives energy, not wisdom. Check whether speed and force help the question.

### 白色 / Albus

- **Core**: whiteness, clarity, wisdom, calm, counsel, purification.
- **Helpful for**: study, advice, healing clarity, intellectual work, peaceful decisions.
- **Difficult for**: passion, speed, force, messy practical struggle.
- **Reading tone**: clear, cool, thoughtful, sober.
- **Misread warning**: Calm can become detachment or inaction if the chart needs initiative.

### 红色 / Rubeus

- **Core**: redness, passion, disruption, danger, intoxication, excess.
- **Helpful for**: exposure, rupture, intense transformation, identifying corruption or volatility.
- **Difficult for**: stable outcomes, calm judgment, health, peace, clean agreements.
- **Reading tone**: volatile, extreme, morally or emotionally heated.
- **Misread warning**: Treat Rubeus carefully. It often warns that the question, situation, or method may be distorted by excess.

### 获得 / Acquisitio

- **Core**: gain, acquisition, profit, increase, accumulation.
- **Helpful for**: money, obtaining, buying, growth, receiving, collecting.
- **Difficult for**: losing, releasing, ending, reducing burden.
- **Reading tone**: something comes in or increases.
- **Misread warning**: Gain is not always desirable. In illness, debt, trouble, or unwanted responsibility, increase may be harmful.

### 失去 / Amissio

- **Core**: loss, release, expense, sacrifice, letting go.
- **Helpful for**: selling, losing a burden, ending attachment, spending appropriately, release.
- **Difficult for**: profit, recovery, retention, keeping possessions.
- **Reading tone**: something goes out, is lost, or is willingly/unwillingly released.
- **Misread warning**: Loss can be fortunate if the question asks to remove, sell, purge, or escape something.

### 龙首 / Caput Draconis

- **Core**: dragon's head, entrance, beginning, threshold, growth, opening.
- **Helpful for**: starting, entering, initiation, arrival, increase, new opportunities.
- **Difficult for**: endings, departure, closing a matter, final release.
- **Reading tone**: a door opens or a new phase begins.
- **Misread warning**: Beginning is not the same as completion. Check whether the chart supports follow-through.

### 龙尾 / Cauda Draconis

- **Core**: dragon's tail, exit, ending, departure, decrease, release.
- **Helpful for**: finishing, leaving, banishing, closure, cutting off old conditions.
- **Difficult for**: beginnings, growth, entry, stable increase.
- **Reading tone**: something exits, ends, or drains away.
- **Misread warning**: Cauda is not automatically bad. It can be exactly right when the desired outcome is closure or removal.

## Pair Logic

Use paired figures to teach contrast:

- **道路 / 群众**: motion versus collective stillness or reflection.
- **大吉 / 小吉**: durable inner fortune versus temporary external fortune.
- **获得 / 失去**: gain versus loss; increase versus release.
- **结合 / 限制**: connection and crossing versus containment and enclosure.
- **喜悦 / 悲伤**: rising joy versus descending heaviness.
- **女人 / 男人**: harmonizing softness versus martial force.
- **白色 / 红色**: clear coolness versus hot volatility.
- **龙首 / 龙尾**: entrance and beginning versus exit and ending.

## Context Rules

- For money questions, Acquisitio, Fortuna Major, Fortuna Minor, and Laetitia often help; Amissio may help only when spending, selling, or losing a liability is desired.
- For love questions, Puella, Coniunctio, Laetitia, and favorable Fortuna figures often help; Puer can show passion or conflict depending on dignity and contact.
- For conflict questions, Puer can help if the querent needs attack or courage, but harm if peace is sought.
- For travel questions, Via and Caput Draconis often help; Carcer often blocks; Cauda Draconis may indicate departure or loss depending on the question.
- For healing questions, Albus, Fortuna Major, Laetitia, and release from harmful figures may help; Rubeus, Tristitia, Carcer, or Acquisitio can be difficult depending on what increases or is bound.
- For endings, Cauda Draconis, Amissio, Carcer, or Tristitia may be useful if the question seeks closure.

## Correspondence Checklist

When making a figure profile, include only what is known from the source or standard table being used:

- Point pattern.
- Basic image.
- Traditional quality or fortune.
- Elemental tendency.
- Planetary association.
- Zodiacal or lunar-mansion association.
- Direction.
- Body or subtle-body correspondence.
- Personality or social role.
- House-specific behavior.
